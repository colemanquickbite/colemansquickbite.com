Meet the owners

Derrick & Shareta ColemanKosciusko, Mississippi

Coleman’s Quick Bite LLC brings comforting, flavorful food to tables throughout Kosciusko, Mississippi and the surrounding area.

Owned by Derrick and Shareta Coleman of Kosciusko, Coleman’s Quick Bite serves breakfast, BBQ, and full spreads for life’s meaningful moments—the kind of food people are happy to gather around.
