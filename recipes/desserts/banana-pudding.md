# Banana Pudding

## Batch Size

1 full-size catering pan

## Ingredients

- 4 boxes instant vanilla pudding mix
- 2 small boxes instant banana cream pudding mix
- 8 cups cold whole milk
- 2 cans sweetened condensed milk, 14 oz each
- 32 oz whipped topping (Cool Whip)
- 2 to 3 tsp vanilla flavoring or vanilla extract
- 10 to 12 bananas, sliced
- 2 boxes vanilla wafer cookies

## Instructions

1. In a large bowl, whisk the vanilla pudding mix, banana cream pudding mix, and cold whole milk until smooth and beginning to thicken.
2. Mix in the sweetened condensed milk and vanilla flavoring.
3. Fold in the Cool Whip until the mixture is smooth, light, and creamy.
4. Place a layer of vanilla wafers in the bottom of the full-size catering pan.
5. Add a layer of sliced bananas.
6. Spread a layer of pudding mixture over the bananas.
7. Repeat the layers until the pan is filled.
8. Finish with pudding mixture on top.
9. Add vanilla wafers on top for garnish.
10. Cover and refrigerate until thoroughly chilled before serving.

## Notes

- **32 oz Cool Whip is the standard amount for one full pan.**
- Use additional Cool Whip if you want the pudding even lighter and fluffier; **40 oz** is an option for an extra-fluffy version.
- Add the top vanilla wafers closer to serving time if you want them to stay firmer.
- Keep refrigerated until service.
