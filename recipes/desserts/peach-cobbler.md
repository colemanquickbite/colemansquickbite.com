# Southern Peach Cobbler

## Batch Size

1 full catering pan

## Ingredients

### Peach Filling

- 1 can sliced peaches, 107 oz
- Additional peaches as desired for a fuller pan
- 2 cups granulated sugar
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 2 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1 tsp ground nutmeg
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- 2 tbsp cornstarch

### Batter

- 1 1/2 cups butter, melted
- 3 cups self-rising flour
- 3 cups whole milk
- 3 cups granulated sugar

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to **350°F**.
2. Drain some of the syrup from the peaches, keeping enough liquid to help form the filling.
3. In a large pot, combine the peaches, granulated sugar, brown sugar, cinnamon, nutmeg, vanilla, lemon juice, and cornstarch.
4. Cook over medium heat until hot and slightly thickened.
5. Pour the melted butter into the bottom of a full-size catering pan.
6. In a separate bowl, mix the self-rising flour, sugar, and whole milk until smooth.
7. Pour the batter evenly over the melted butter. Do not stir.
8. Spoon the hot peach mixture evenly over the batter. Do not stir.
9. Bake uncovered at **350°F for about 50–65 minutes**, or until the crust is golden brown and the filling is bubbling.
10. Let rest for **15–20 minutes** before serving.

## Notes

- One 107 oz can works as the base amount for one full pan.
- Add more peaches if you want a heavier fruit layer.
- If adding a lot more peaches, slightly increase the cinnamon, vanilla, brown sugar, and cornstarch as needed.
- Do not add all of the canned syrup automatically; too much liquid can make the cobbler runny.
- Place the pan on a sheet pan while baking in case the filling bubbles over.
- The cobbler will thicken as it cools.
