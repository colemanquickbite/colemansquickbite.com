# Fried Party Wings

## Standard Batch

Catering batch

## Ingredients

- Party wings
- Frying oil
- Kosher salt
- Coarse black pepper
- Garlic powder
- Onion powder
- Small amount of cornstarch for extra crispiness

## Production Notes

- Season wings with kosher salt, coarse black pepper, garlic powder, and onion powder.
- Lightly coat or dust with a small amount of cornstarch before frying.
- Fry in batches until fully cooked and crisp.
- Drain well before seasoning or saucing.
- Frying oil should be included in food-cost calculations when purchased specifically for an event.
- If oil is already on hand, note it as existing inventory rather than assigning the full purchase cost to one event.

## Flavor Options

- Traditional Fried
- Honey BBQ
- Hot Honey
- Dry Lemon Pepper

## Catering Costing Notes

Track:

- Cost per case or bag of party wings
- Total pounds purchased
- Approximate wing count per pound
- Total wings required for the event
- Frying oil cost
- Cornstarch cost
- Sauce or seasoning cost
- Estimated food cost per guest
