# Wood-Fire Smoked Pork Spare Ribs

## Standard Batch

1 slab pork spare ribs

## Ingredients

- 1 slab pork spare ribs
- Kosher salt
- Coarse black pepper
- Onion powder
- Garlic powder
- Heath Riles Sweet BBQ Rub
- Charcoal
- Split dry smoking wood:
  - Oak
  - Pecan
  - Cherry
  - Use whichever is dry and available

## Preparation

1. Trim ribs as needed.
2. Season with kosher salt, coarse black pepper, onion powder, garlic powder, and Heath Riles Sweet BBQ Rub.
3. No binder is used.
4. Smoke over charcoal and split dry wood.
5. Use oak, pecan, cherry, or a combination depending on what is available.

## Notes

- No mustard binder.
- No foil wrapping.
- Wood choice may vary based on availability.
- Charcoal and smoking wood should be included when calculating total production cost.
- Record rib cost by slab for catering quote calculations.
