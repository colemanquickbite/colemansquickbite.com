# Wood-Fire Smoked Pulled Pork

## Standard Batch

1 pork butt / Boston butt

## Ingredients

- 1 pork butt or Boston butt
- Kosher salt
- Coarse black pepper
- Garlic powder
- Onion powder
- Heath Riles Sweet BBQ Rub
- Charcoal
- Split dry smoking wood:
  - Oak
  - Pecan
  - Cherry
  - Use whichever is dry and available
- BBQ sauce, if used for serving

## Preparation

1. Trim excess fat as needed.
2. Season with kosher salt, coarse black pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, and Heath Riles Sweet BBQ Rub.
3. Smoke over charcoal and split dry wood until tender enough to pull.
4. Rest the pork before shredding.
5. Pull or shred the pork and remove excess fat as needed.
6. Add BBQ sauce only if desired for serving.

## Notes

- No binder required.
- Wood choice may vary depending on what is dry and available.
- Track pork cost by weight.
- Track charcoal, smoking wood, seasoning, and BBQ sauce separately for catering cost calculations.
- Record finished cooked yield when possible so future quotes can estimate cost per pound of usable pulled pork.
