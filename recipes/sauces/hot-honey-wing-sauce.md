# Hot Honey Wing Sauce

## Standard Batch

Working batch for party wings

## Ingredients

- 1 cup hot sauce
- 2 cups honey
- 2 tbsp butter
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1/2 tsp coarse black pepper
- 1 tbsp cornstarch
- 1 tbsp cold water
- Optional: cayenne pepper for additional heat

## Instructions

1. Add the hot sauce and 2 cups of honey to a saucepan.
2. Stir in the garlic powder, onion powder, and black pepper.
3. Warm over low to medium-low heat.
4. Add the butter and stir until melted.
5. In a small cup, mix the cornstarch with the cold water until smooth.
6. Slowly stir the cornstarch slurry into the warm sauce.
7. Continue heating and stirring until the sauce slightly thickens and becomes glossy.
8. Taste the sauce.
9. Add additional honey as needed until the sweetness and heat are balanced to your liking.
10. Toss with hot fried wings until evenly coated.

## Notes

- 2 cups honey is the starting amount.
- Add more honey gradually and taste as you go.
- The finished sauce should be sweet, spicy, and thick enough to cling to the wings.
- Do not add dry cornstarch directly to the sauce; always mix it with cold water first.
- Do not over-thicken. The sauce will thicken more as it cools.
- Add cayenne if additional heat is desired.
- Sauce can be made ahead and reheated gently.
