# Honey BBQ Wing Sauce

## Standard Batch

Working batch for party wings

## Ingredients

- 2 cups BBQ sauce
- 1 cup honey
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 2 tbsp butter, optional

## Instructions

1. Add BBQ sauce and honey to a saucepan.
2. Stir in the garlic powder and onion powder.
3. Warm over low heat, stirring until fully combined.
4. Add butter if using and stir until melted.
5. Toss with hot fried wings until evenly coated.

## Notes

- This version uses a heavier honey ratio for a sweeter, stickier glaze.
- Add a little more honey if you want an even stronger honey flavor.
- If the BBQ sauce is already very sweet, reduce the honey slightly.
- Sauce can be made ahead and reheated gently.
- Track BBQ sauce, honey, and seasonings separately for catering cost calculations.
