# Seasoned String Beans with Smoked Turkey

## Batch Size

1 full catering pan

## Ingredients

- 2 cans Great Value Cut Green Beans, 101 oz each
- Smoked turkey
- Seasoning blend diced vegetables
- Chicken broth or stock
- Butter
- Kosher salt
- Coarse black pepper
- Garlic powder
- Onion powder

## Notes

- Standard batch uses **2 × 101 oz cans** of green beans.
- Estimated yield based on the supplied serving count: **48 half-cup servings** of green beans before adding smoked turkey, seasoning blend, and cooking liquid.
- For 50 guests, start with 2 cans and buy a third can as backup if generous portions are expected.
- Do not automatically add all 3 cans to one pan.
- Quantities for the remaining ingredients and cooking instructions have not yet been recorded.
