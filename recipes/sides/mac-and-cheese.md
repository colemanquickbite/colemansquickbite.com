# Southern Creamy Baked Mac & Cheese

## Batch Size

1 full catering pan

## Ingredients

- 3 lb elbow macaroni
- 1.5 qt heavy cream
- 12 oz evaporated milk
- 2 cups whole milk
- 1 stick butter
- 2 lb Colby & Monterey Jack cheese
- 8 oz Velveeta
- 8 oz mozzarella
- Garlic powder, to taste
- Onion powder, to taste
- Black pepper, to taste
- Salt, to taste
- Optional smoked paprika or cayenne

## Instructions

1. Cook the macaroni until just tender, slightly under al dente. Do not overcook.
2. Drain and set aside.
3. In a large pot, warm the heavy cream, evaporated milk, whole milk, butter, and Velveeta over low to medium-low heat.
4. Stir until the butter and Velveeta are melted and the mixture is smooth.
5. Season with garlic powder, onion powder, black pepper, and salt.
6. Add most of the Colby & Monterey Jack cheese and mozzarella, reserving some cheese for the top.
7. Stir until the cheese is melted and the sauce is creamy.
8. Fold the cooked macaroni into the cheese sauce.
9. Transfer to one full-size catering pan.
10. Top with the reserved shredded cheese.
11. Cover with foil and bake at **325°F for about 35–40 minutes**.
12. Remove the foil and bake another **15–20 minutes**, or until hot, bubbly, and lightly browned on top.

## Notes

- 3 lb of dry elbow macaroni should comfortably fill one full catering pan once combined with the cheese sauce. For most full hotel pans, 2.5–3 lb dry pasta is a good range for a creamy result that is not packed too tightly.
- This version omits cream cheese and uses 12 oz evaporated milk, with whole milk to keep the sauce loose and creamy.
- Start with **2 cups whole milk** and only add more if needed.
- If the mixture looks too thick before baking, add another **½ to 1 cup whole milk**.
- The macaroni should look slightly looser than you want before baking because it will absorb liquid as it cooks.
- Can be assembled the night before and refrigerated.
- If baking directly from the refrigerator, allow additional baking time.
