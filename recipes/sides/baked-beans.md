# Southern Baked Beans

## Batch Size

1 full catering pan

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 large cans baked beans, about 6–7 lb total
- 2 lb ground beef
- 1 to 1 1/2 cups seasoning blend diced vegetables
- 3 cups BBQ sauce
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 1/4 to 1/2 cup pancake syrup or table syrup
- 2 tbsp yellow mustard
- 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- Optional: 1/2 tsp smoked paprika

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to **350°F**.
2. Brown the ground beef in a large skillet or pot.
3. Add the seasoning blend diced vegetables and cook until softened.
4. Drain excess grease if needed.
5. Combine the baked beans, cooked ground beef, and seasoning blend.
6. Add the BBQ sauce, brown sugar, syrup, mustard, Worcestershire sauce, garlic powder, onion powder, black pepper, and smoked paprika if using.
7. Mix well and taste before baking.
8. Pour into one full-size catering pan.
9. Cover with foil and bake for **35–40 minutes**.
10. Remove foil and bake another **15–20 minutes**, until hot and bubbling around the edges.
11. Let rest about **10 minutes** before serving.

## Notes

- **3 cups BBQ sauce** is the standard amount for one full pan.
- **1 cup brown sugar** is the standard sweetener amount.
- Start with **1/4 cup syrup** and add more if needed.
- No ketchup.
- No bacon.
- Can be prepared ahead, refrigerated, and reheated before service.
