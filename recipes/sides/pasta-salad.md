# Pasta Salad — 1 Full Pan

## Batch Size

1 full catering pan

## Ingredients

- 3 lb rotini pasta
- 1 1/4 to 1 1/2 bottles Olive Garden Italian Dressing
- 1/4 cup mayonnaise
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1/2 tsp coarse black pepper
- Diced vegetables as desired
- Shredded cheese, if used

## Instructions

1. Cook the rotini until just tender.
2. Drain and rinse with cold water.
3. Cool completely.
4. Add vegetables and cheese.
5. Start with about **1 1/4 bottles** of Olive Garden dressing.
6. Add mayonnaise and mix well.
7. Add **2 tbsp sugar** and seasonings.
8. Refrigerate for at least 1–2 hours.
9. Stir and taste again after chilling.
10. Add more dressing a little at a time, up to about **1 1/2 bottles total**, if needed.

## Notes

- Do not start with 2 full bottles. Olive Garden dressing is strong and tangy.
- The small amount of mayonnaise softens the acidity without making it taste like a mayonnaise-based pasta salad.
- Start with only 2 tbsp sugar. Add more only 1 tbsp at a time after tasting.
- Pasta absorbs dressing while chilling, so always taste again before serving.
