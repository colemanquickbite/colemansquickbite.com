# Loaded Mashed Potatoes

## Batch Size

1 full catering pan

## Ingredients

- Potatoes
- Butter
- Whole milk
- Heavy cream, optional
- Sour cream
- Shredded cheddar cheese
- Bacon bits or cooked bacon, if used
- Green onions or chives, if used
- Kosher salt
- Coarse black pepper
- Garlic powder
- Onion powder

## Instructions

1. Peel and cut potatoes into evenly sized pieces.
2. Boil until fork-tender.
3. Drain thoroughly.
4. Mash with butter, whole milk, and sour cream.
5. Season with kosher salt, coarse black pepper, garlic powder, and onion powder.
6. Fold in shredded cheddar cheese.
7. Add bacon and green onions or chives if using.
8. Transfer to one full-size catering pan.
9. Top with additional cheese if desired.
10. Keep warm for service or cover and refrigerate until reheating.

## Notes

- Add milk gradually so the potatoes do not become too loose.
- Keep the potatoes slightly creamier than the final desired texture because they will tighten while holding.
- Track potatoes, butter, milk, sour cream, cheese, and toppings separately for catering cost calculations.
- Exact quantities should be updated to match the normal Coleman’s Quick Bite full-pan batch.
